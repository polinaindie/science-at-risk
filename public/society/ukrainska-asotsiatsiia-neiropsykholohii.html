Ukrainian Association of Neuropsychology

An association of mental health professionals for communication, cooperation and support.

Keywords:

Basic information:

The Ukrainian Association of Neuropsychology was created to bring together psychologists, psychotherapists, doctors, speech therapists, and dermatologists for scientific and practical cooperation in applying the neuropsychological approach to correctional, therapeutic, and counseling work in the mental health of children and adults.

Regions of active representation or activity:

Kyiv

Main projects of the company:

Establishing a joint forum with Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv to disseminate educational and useful material in the field of neuropsychology.

Publishing thematic monographs.

Holding online conferences, live broadcasts and thematic meetings within the Association's sections.
