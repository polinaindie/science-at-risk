Iuliia Motuzka

Doctor of Technical Science, Professor

Kyiv National University of Trade and Economics

  • Economics

  • Food science

  • Science popularization

  • Entrepreneurship and Trade

  • Commodity science

  • Identification of goods

  • Production and technology

Working languages

Ukrainian, German, English, Russian

Research field

Iulia's areas of interest include food safety and quality, expert examination of goods and services, commodity science, product identification, and consumer protection. She promotes Ukrainian science through contacts and joint research with international partners.

Duties

Iuliia is engaged in developing food products for military personnel, patients (enteral nutrition), and food products for people with increased physical and psycho-emotional stress, and also research the issues of technical regulation, consumer protection, and comparative testing of goods and services.

She is a professor at the Department of Safety and Quality Management at the State University of Trade and Economics.
