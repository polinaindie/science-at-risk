Research field

Mykola Lychuk’s research interests cover the following areas:

1. Metabolic diseases in cattle: studying the prevalence and structure of metabolic disorders in high-producing cows, including osteodystrophy, ketosis, hepatosis, and other non-infectious diseases caused by metabolic disturbances.

2. Impact of micronutrient deficiency and technogenic pollution: studying the effects of deficiencies in micronutrients such as cobalt, selenium, and zinc, as well as excess levels of heavy metals such as cadmium and lead, on the antioxidant system, erythropoiesis, and the overall resistance of cows, particularly in the Carpathian region and technologically polluted areas.

3. Animal gastroenterology: abomasoenteritis in calves, colic in horses, diseases of the digestive tract, and the development of methods for their correction and treatment.

4. Prevention and treatment of non-infectious animal diseases: development and testing of therapeutic and preventive protocols, including the use of hepatoprotective agents, micronutrient preparations, and premixes in different animal species.

5. Clinical veterinary laboratory diagnostics: conducting hematological and biochemical analyses of blood and other biological fluids. Work with automated hematology and biochemistry analyzers, as well as urine analyzers.

Duties

Mykola Lychuk is an Associate Professor at the Department of Internal Animal Diseases and Clinical Diagnostics at Stepan Gzhytskyi National University of Veterinary Medicine and Biotechnologies of Lviv.

He combines teaching and research activities, with his work focused on internal veterinary medicine.
