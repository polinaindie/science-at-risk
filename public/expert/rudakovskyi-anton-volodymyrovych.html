Research field

Anton researches cosmology, dark matter, the first galaxies, epochs of reionization, and the Dark Ages in the Universe.

Duties

Anton is a researcher at the Bogolyubov Institute of Theoretical Physics. He is interested in studying the nature of dark matter and the epoch of formation of the first galaxies in the Universe.

Experience

During 2015-2018, Anton was a postgraduate student at the Bogolyubov Institute of Theoretical Physics of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine. After that, during 2018-2020, he held the position of leading engineer. Since 2021, he has worked as a junior researcher at the Institute of Theoretical Physics of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine.

Since 2019, Anton has also been a member of the Council of Young Scientists of the Department of Physics and Astronomy of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine.
