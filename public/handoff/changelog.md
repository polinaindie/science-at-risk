# What changed in the hand-off, 2026-11-17 to 2026-11-25

A reading list for the developers: what moved since the package was first handed over. Each line names the file to read.

## Type and sizes
- **One size scale** (`--step-*`, generated by `scripts/build-type-scale.py`) now drives the site, the static pages and the cabinet. Reading text 18 phone / 20 desktop. `typography.md` has the table. A font audit (`docs/font-audit/`) lists every element and its step.
- **Wide screens, 1920px and up:** text, headings, cabinet and form fields grow by a fifth.
- **Buttons:** contact buttons are 48px high at every desktop width.
- **Header, footer, tabs, wordmark, mobile menu** take sizes from the scale; the language switch stays on the right at every width; the footer got a rule above the base line, copyright left and the OpenTech wordmark right.
- **Fields:** an error state (the underline stays black, the message is yellow on black under the field, `aria-invalid`), in the React component and the Hugo partial; typed text at read size, hints 14px.

## Pages
- **Stories:** type label "Feature"; lead of three sentences; captions; photo gallery and video in articles; sticky right-column menu from three sections up (`content-model/story.md`).
- **About:** key figures, directions list, team cards with photos, in-page menu (`content-model/page.md`).
- **Research:** date above the title, lede and cards on the scale (`content-model/research.md`).
- **Infrastructure:** amounts in USD, plate and page on the scale, breadcrumbs from three steps, e-mail line removed (`content-model/institution.md`).
- **Societies and expert page:** "Write to …" button with a modal (`content-model/society.md`, `content-model/scientist.md`).
- **Home:** responsive pass on phone and tablet, block text steps up from 1920px.

## Cabinet
- Notifications as a flat list, collaboration with a lede and "Start a conversation", opportunities with a description, profile with pencil edit and unsent-changes bar (`cabinet/README.md`, "The React cabinet").
- Dates `dd.mm.yy` everywhere.

## Text and data
- Two keys added to `i18n/` (`stories.toc_title`, `society.write_to`), `stories.type_stories` is "Feature" in EN; 314 keys in both files.
- `needed_uah` is now `needed_usd` in `data/institution.yaml` and the content model.
- Three stories had Cyrillic letters typed inside English words: fixed.

## Open questions added
42 to 48 in `open-questions.md` (USD rate, wide-screen threshold, the two menu scripts, lead, contact form, cabinet data, the Feature name). 36, 39 and 40 changed status.

## Process
