Component states

The components of the live site with their states. Buttons, links, tags, fields, scope switch, header and footer are rendered from src/components, the markup the static pages carry. Cards, breadcrumbs, search, pagination and the Connect form are cut out of the static pages themselves: see static-components.html. Hover and focus are the real pseudo-classes: move the pointer, press Tab. The last two sections are proposals that the live site does not have yet.

Buttons (live component)

Rendered from src/components/Button. The shape is data-squircle + js/satr-shape.js; hover and focus are real (move the pointer, press Tab).

Text links (live component)

Rendered from src/components/Link.

Chips and tags (live component)

Rendered from src/components/Tag. A chip is a button (filter) or a link; the label form is for editorial rubrics.

Fields (live component)

Rendered from src/components/TextField: floating label. The label rides up on focus or when the field has text. A field has no focus ring of its own: the label and the caret show where you are. With error the underline stays as it is and the message stands under the field on the attention yellow; the field is marked aria-invalid.

Navigation (live component)

Rendered from src/components/SiteHeader, the header of every page: wordmark, EN/UK switch, Log in, menu button. The menu opens full screen (press the button; Esc closes it). The language switch links to the same page in the other language (hreflang); the current language is aria-current=true.

Footer (live component)

Rendered from src/components/Footer, the footer of every page: the ask, the form (with its count and over-limit message), the supporters and the implementer, then the base line with the copyright and the OpenTech wordmark. Ukrainian below.

Scope switch (live component)

Rendered from src/components/ScopeTabs, links form.
