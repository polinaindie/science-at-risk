  • Razet Basnukaeva

    More details

    Ph.D.

    B.Verkin Institute for Low Temperature Physics and Engineering of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine

    Senior Research Scientist at the Department of Thermal Properties and Structure of Solids and Nanosystems at the B.Verkin Institute for Low Temperature Physics and Engineering of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine. She works in the field of studying the thermal and sorption properties of the latest nanostructured materials, such as fullerite, carbon nanotubes, graphene oxide and composites based on them. She investigates the influence of physical and technological conditions on their properties and develops physical principles and technologies for creating nanocomposites with improved mechanical characteristics, when the introduction of carbon nanosystems significantly changes the physical and operational qualities of the material.
