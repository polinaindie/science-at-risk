  • Serhіі Khablak

    More details

    Doctor of Sciences, Associate Professor

    Institute of Food Biotechnology and Genomics, NAS of Ukraine

    Serhii Khablak is a researcher in the fields of plant physiology, immunity, and stress responses in agricultural crops. His main research focuses on the molecular mechanisms underlying the resistance of winter wheat and sunflower to biotic and abiotic stresses, the functioning of immune genes and receptors, as well as the critical analysis of modern agricultural technologies, specialty fertilizers, and biostimulants.
